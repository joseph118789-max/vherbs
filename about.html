About VHerbs

VHerbs is a Malaysia-based producer of halal-certified herbal and spice products. We started with a simple belief: that the things you put in your body should be made by people who care about what's in them.

We produce a growing range of premium herbal and spice blends for household and wholesale customers. Every product is packed under MS1500 halal conditions, with full traceability from incoming ingredient to outgoing carton.

Our facility includes dedicated receiving, processing, packing, and finished-goods storage zones — kept physically separate to prevent cross-contamination. Staff wear full PPE when entering production areas, and visitors are escorted at all times.

Our certifications

We hold ourselves to standards that are independently audited. Our halal certification is renewed annually and covers our full production chain.

Malaysia Halal

Certified to Malaysian Standard MS1500 — the national standard for halal food production. Our certification covers ingredient sourcing, processing, packing, storage and dispatch.

Cert #1 028-06/2025

What MS1500 covers

  • Segregation of halal and non-halal ingredients at every stage
  • Documented sourcing with supplier traceability
  • Cleaning and sanitation procedures for all food-contact surfaces
  • Staff training and PPE for production areas
  • Independent audit by the Department of Islamic Development Malaysia (JAKIM)
